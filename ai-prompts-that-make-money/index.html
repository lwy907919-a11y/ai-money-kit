You want prompts that actually make money, not "write a poem about a cat" prompts. Here are 15 prompts that turn AI into a money-making tool. All copy-paste ready.

For Freelancers (Resume / VA services)

Prompt 1 — Resume Optimizer

You are a senior recruiter with 10 years at FAANG. Optimize this resume for ATS:
[Paste resume]
Target role: [role]
Target company: [company]
Deliver: optimized version + 5 changes explained + interview prep.

Prompt 2 — Client Email Drafter

Draft a professional email to [client name] about [topic]. Tone: [formal/friendly/direct]. Length: 100-150 words. Include clear call to action.

For Content Creators

Prompt 3 — Viral Hook Generator

Give me 10 viral hooks for an article about [topic]. Each hook must stop the scroll in 3 seconds. Mix: curiosity, controversy, story, statistic.

Prompt 4 — Article Outline

Outline a 1500-word article on [topic]. Audience: [who]. Goal: [educate/persuade/convert]. Structure: H2 sections with 3 H3 subsections each. Include 1 case study + 3 quotable stats.

Prompt 5 — Social Post Repurposer

Turn this article into 5 tweets + 1 LinkedIn post + 1 Instagram caption + 1 TikTok script. Match each platform's native style.
[Paste article]

For E-commerce Sellers

Prompt 6 — Amazon Listing

Write an Amazon listing for [product]. Include: SEO title (200 chars), 5 bullet points (focus on benefits), 1000-char description, 7 backend search terms. Target keyword: [keyword].

Prompt 7 — Customer Review Response

A customer left a [1-5 star] review saying: "[review]". Write a public response that addresses the concern, stays on-brand, and turns it into a positive moment.

For Service Providers (Coaches, Consultants, Agencies)

Prompt 8 — Lead Magnet Idea

Generate 10 lead magnet ideas for [my business]. Each must: solve 1 specific problem, take < 10 minutes to consume, lead to my paid offer. Format: title + 1-sentence description.

Prompt 9 — Discovery Call Prep

I have a discovery call with [prospect name, role, company]. Their stated problem: [problem]. Give me: 10 questions to ask (open-ended), 3 hypotheses about their real problem, 3 case studies I should mention.

Prompt 10 — Proposal Generator

Write a 1-page proposal for [service]. Client: [name]. Problem: [problem]. My solution: [solution]. Price: $X. Include: scope, timeline, deliverables, 1 testimonial, clear next step.

For Product Builders

Prompt 11 — Landing Page Copy

Write landing page copy for [product]. Target: [audience]. Benefit: [main benefit]. Include: hero (headline + sub + CTA), 3 features with benefits, social proof section, FAQ, final CTA.

Prompt 12 — App Store Description

Write an App Store description for [app]. Subtitle (30 chars), full description (under 4000 chars), 170-char promotional text. Include 5 keywords. Style: clear, benefit-first, 1 CTA.

For Sales / Outreach

Prompt 13 — Cold Email

Write a cold email to [name, role, company]. Subject line (5 words max). Body: 75 words. Hook in line 1, value in lines 2-3, soft CTA in line 4. No fluff, no "just checking in".

Prompt 14 — Follow-up Sequence

Write a 4-email follow-up sequence (Day 3, 7, 14, 30) to [name] who didn't reply to my cold email about [topic]. Each email: 50-75 words, different angle, no "just bumping this up".

Prompt 15 — Objection Handler

A prospect said: "[objection]". They are [context]. Respond in 3 sentences: acknowledge, reframe, redirect. Tone: confident, not defensive. End with question to advance the conversation.
