Common Mistakes (And How to Avoid Them)

1. Trying to do everything at once

Fix: Pick ONE service from the Kit. Master it. Add the next one in month 2.

2. Pricing too low

Fix: The Kit's pricing guides tell you what's normal. If you're undercharging, raise rates 50% this week.

3. Waiting for "perfect"

Fix: Launch with what you have. 3 samples is enough. Iterate based on client feedback.

4. No social proof

Fix: After every client, ask for a testimonial. Use the first 3 in your marketing.

5. Giving up after week 1

Fix: Most people don't make money until month 2-3. Stay consistent for 90 days.

What If It Doesn't Work?

If after 30 days you haven't made $1,000, ask yourself:

  1. Did I post daily? Most people under-distribute. Aim for 5 posts/day on social.
  2. Did I charge? If you're not charging, you're not in business.
  3. Did I deliver well? Bad first reviews kill momentum. Over-deliver on the first 3.
  4. Did I pick the right service? Some services are easier to sell than others. Resume is easiest, chatbot is hardest.

If after 60 days still $0 → switch to a different service from the Kit. There are 5 to choose from.
